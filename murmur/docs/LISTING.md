## Description

**Capture on your phone. Think with your own AI. Pay once.**

Murmur turns any conversation — a meeting, a lecture, a phone call on speaker, or a quick voice note — into a clean, searchable, summarized note. And it does it without a subscription and without sending your audio to anyone.

**Unlimited, private transcription.** Murmur transcribes entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech engine. No minute limits, no meter, no upload. Your recordings and transcripts stay on your device.

**Summaries from the AI you choose.** Out of the box, Murmur uses Apple's free on-device model — no account, no key, no cost. Want more power on long or complex transcripts? Plug in your own Claude or OpenAI key and Murmur uses it directly from your phone. Your key, your bill, your choice.

**Notes shaped your way.** Meeting minutes, action items, interview notes, lecture study notes, a cleaned-up voice memo, a ready-to-send follow-up email — pick a template, or write your own in plain English. Every template is editable. Nothing is locked behind a paywall.

**Ask your notes.** Chat with any recording — "What did we decide?", "List every action item and who owns it" — and get answers grounded in the transcript, with quotes.

**Works with Shortcuts & Siri.** "Summarize my last recording." "Draft a follow-up email." Turn a recording into a finished thing without opening the app.

**No subscription. Ever.** Buy Murmur once. Optional one-time template packs add tools for specific work — sales calls, clinical notes, journalism — but the core app is yours to keep.

**Why Murmur instead of a $170 gadget + monthly plan?**
Because your iPhone already has the microphone, the transcription, and — with Apple Intelligence — the AI. Murmur just puts them together, beautifully, and gets out of your way.

Private by design. On-device by default. Yours to own.

## App Privacy answers (App Store Connect)
- **Data collection:** None. Murmur has no backend and no analytics.
- **Audio & transcripts:** Stored only on the user's device.
- **Third-party AI:** If the user configures their own Claude/OpenAI key, the
  transcript text they choose to summarize is sent from their device to that
  provider under the user's own account. Disclose this as "Data Not Collected by
  the developer; user-initiated requests to a third-party AI provider."

## Review notes (for App Review)
Murmur works fully offline with Apple's on-device model — reviewers do not need
an API key to test core functionality. To test the optional bring-your-own-key
path, add any valid Claude or OpenAI API key in Settings. In-app purchases are
one-time non-consumable template packs (no subscriptions).
